# Hi, I'm Lut 👋

I'm a Civil Engineer with 13 years in construction — precast concrete 
manufacturing, light rail track slab, civil works for maintenance facilities, 
and most recently heavy rail construction and maintenance.

Frankly, the work is no longer as exciting for me. It's time to adapt to what 
the future will inevitably bring to the industry: productivity increase through 
Artificial Intelligence. I can't remain ignorant of the tsunami of change that 
will eventually hit construction, and I want to make sure I utilise the 
opportunities early enough to maintain a competitive edge.

## What I'm building

- *BCAT* — A ballast cleaning scheduling app for heavy-haul rail maintenance. 
  It replaces a manual, multi-engineer planning process with an app that auto-
  generates time-distance diagrams and runs optimisation scenarios to maximise 
  scope within a given working window.
- *Network Agent* (planned) — A natural-language interface for querying 
  rail network asset data, built on RAG.
- *Project Intelligence* (planned) — Cross-project status querying for 
  construction projects.

## Why I'm here

I'm not a software engineer, so I couldn't have built these tools myself even 
five years ago. With AI-assisted development, I can. My aim is to use my 
construction knowledge to develop process-specific applications for small and 
large companies — and to build skills along the way.
